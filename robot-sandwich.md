
# A Robot's Step-by-Step guide to make a PB&J Sandwich

## Needed Items
- Table
- Butter knife
- Napkin
- Loaf of Bread
- Jar of Jelly
- Jar of Peanut Butter

## Assumptions
- The table is clean.
- The robot's hands are clean.
- When the instructions state to place an Item on the table, without any further details, it means place it in it's original position.
- Everything that is done, will be done with your hands.

## Instructions
1. Reach out and grab the packaged loaf of bread.
2. Open the packaged loaf of bread by untying the knot keeping it sealed, and spread the wrapping open.
3. Reach inside the bag and grab one slice of bread.
4. Take out the slice and place it flat out on the table.
5. Grab the Jar of Peanut Butter.
6. Twist open the cap to the Jar, and place it down on the table to the left of the slice of bread.
7. Place the Peanut Butter Jar back on the table, to the right of the slice of bread 
8. Grab the butter knife.
9. Using the butter knife scoop up some of the peanut butter inside of the jar, keep the jar stable by holding it in place with your free hand, then spread the peanut butter all over the face that is visible on the slice of bread.
10. Repeat the last step until the peanut butter is evenly spread out on the slice of bread. ONLY repeat if the peanut butter is not evenly spread out.
11. Once done put down the butter knife on the napkin.
12. Pick up and grab both the cap to the peanut butter jar and the jar itself; twist the cap back onto the jar, Sealing it. Then place it back on the table.
13. Reach inside the bag of bread and grab a different slice of bread.
14. Take out and place that new slice of bread flat out on the table right next to the first slice of bread, to the right.
15. Retie the package for the loaf of bread and place it on the table.
16. Grab the Jar of Jelly.
17. Twist open the cap to the Jar, and place it down on the table to the left of the original slice of bread.
18. Place the Jelly Jar back on the table, to the right of this new slice of bread. 
19. Grab both the butter knife and the napkin, wipe the peanut butter off the butter knife then place the napkin down on the table.
20. Using the butter knife scoop up some of the Jelly inside of the jar, keep the jar stable by holding it in place with your free hand, then spread the Jelly all over the face that is visible on the new slice of bread.
21. Repeat the last step until the Jelly is evenly spread out on the new slice of bread. ONLY repeat if the Jelly is not evenly spread out.
22. Once done put down the butter knife on the napkin.
23. Pick up and grab both the cap to the peanut butter jar and the jar itself; twist the cap back onto the jar, Sealing it. Then place it back on the table.
24. Grab both slices of bread on the table, flip over the slice with the Jelly onto the slice with the Peanut Butter, making sure to fully conceal both the jelly and peanut butter in between the slices.

## Done When
- Every item on the table has been resealed and in their original positions.
- The sandwich is complete and all the ingredients being used properly.
